Four Row Tapered Roller Bearings

Tapered Roller Bearings

Four row tapered roller bearings combine very high capacity with shock resistance for rolling-mill roll necks and mine hoisting equipment. Clearance is set by spacers to keep the mill running accurately.

MaterialGCr15 high-carbon chromium steel / carburising steel (optional)
