Needle Roller Bearings

More Bearing Types

Needle roller bearings use thin cylindrical rollers with a small cross-section, giving high radial load capacity in a compact envelope. The NK series runs directly on a hardened, ground shaft as the inner raceway; the NA49 series adds a machined inner ring for shafts that cannot serve as a raceway. Both series suit gearboxes, automotive components, textile machinery and general industrial applications where space is limited.

Series note: NK bearings have no inner ring — the shaft raceway diameter (Fw) is the governing bore dimension, and the shaft must be hardened and ground. NA49 bearings include an inner ring and mount on standard shafts. Caged and full-complement designs are available on request.
