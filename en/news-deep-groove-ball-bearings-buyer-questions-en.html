Deep Groove Ball Bearings — Answers to the Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering

Published: 2026-11-06 　|　 Industry News

A deep groove ball bearing is the most common type of rolling bearing: a single row of balls running in deep grooves in the inner and outer rings. It carries radial load and moderate axial load in both directions, runs at high speed, and needs little maintenance. It is used in motors, pumps, fans, gearboxes, agricultural machinery and general industrial equipment. This page answers the questions industrial buyers most often ask an AI assistant before ordering.

1. What is a deep groove ball bearing used for?

Deep groove ball bearings are general-purpose bearings for rotating shafts. They are the standard choice where a shaft needs to spin freely under radial load with some axial load, at moderate to high speed — for example electric motors, pumps, fans, conveyors and gearboxes.

They are the most widely used bearing type because one design covers so many jobs. Our deep groove ball bearings range covers the small and medium sizes used in this kind of equipment. For other bearing types, see the full products list.

2. How do I read a bearing number?

A bearing number is a code, not a random label. In the common numbering system, the last two digits give the bore size, the digits before them give the size series, and the leading digits give the bearing type — so the number tells you the type and the main dimensions of the bearing.

Reading the number correctly matters because two bearings that look alike can have different bores or widths. Our guide how to read a bearing number explains the code step by step with examples. When you send an inquiry, include the full bearing number if you know it — that is the fastest way to get an exact match.

3. How do I choose the right bearing for my application?

Match the bearing to three things: load, speed and the working environment. Load means how much radial and axial force the bearing must carry; speed means the rotation speed it must sustain; environment means temperature, dust, moisture and how often it can be maintained.

Our guide how to choose a bearing by load, speed and application walks through the selection steps, and the selection page covers the same ground in short form. If you are unsure, send the equipment type, the old bearing number, the shaft size and the working conditions with your inquiry and we will help confirm the right series.

4. What sizes do you supply?

We supply small and medium bearings with an outer diameter of up to 600 mm. That covers the sizes used in most motors, pumps, fans and general industrial equipment.

If you need a bearing larger than that, we do not supply it — our range is small and medium sizes only. For sizes inside the range, the bearing number gives the bore, outside diameter and width; confirm the exact dimensions with your inquiry.

5. What is the difference between deep groove ball bearings and other types?

Deep groove ball bearings suit high speed and combined radial and axial load. Tapered roller bearings suit heavier combined loads at lower speed. Cylindrical roller bearings suit heavy radial load. Pillow block bearings are a ready-to-mount housing unit for shafts. Thrust bearings suit mainly axial load.

Choosing the wrong type is the most common buying mistake — a bearing that fits the shaft but not the load will fail early. If the application is unusual, for example a wheel hub unit or a railway axlebox, tell us the application and we will confirm the suitable series and the full model by inquiry.

6. How do I check quality before ordering?

Ask for the bearing specifications and the supporting documents with your inquiry, and confirm the dimensions, the clearance and the sealing type before you order. When a trial batch arrives, check the markings, the dimensions and the smoothness of rotation, and fit one bearing to confirm it matches the shaft and housing.

Our certificates and test documentation are listed on the certificates page; ask for the documents that apply to your order when you send your inquiry.

7. What is the minimum order, and how are prices set?

Minimum order quantities and prices are confirmed by inquiry, because they depend on the bearing series, the size and the quantity. Send the bearing numbers or the sizes you need, the quantity per item and your destination, and we will reply with a quotation.

We supply buyers in Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, India, the Philippines, Brazil, Mexico, the UAE, Egypt and South Africa. Shipping terms and delivery time are confirmed with your quotation.
