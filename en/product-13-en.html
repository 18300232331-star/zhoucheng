Deep Groove Ball Bearings

Deep Groove Ball Bearings

Simple in design, low in friction and capable of very high speeds, deep groove ball bearings take radial and two-direction axial loads. They are the most widely used bearing type — motors, fans, pumps, agricultural and general machinery.
