CC Series Spherical Roller Bearings

Spherical Roller Bearings

CC series bearings use pressed cages and optimised roller profile crowning for lower friction and temperature rise. Their self-aligning design compensates for shaft deflection and mounting error, suiting wind main shafts, large motors and gearboxes.
