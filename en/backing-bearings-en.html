Backing Bearings

Products · Backing Bearings

Backing bearings are made for the support roll stacks of cluster mills (such as 20-high and 12-high Sendzimir mills). Multi-row cylindrical rollers and a thick-walled outer ring give extremely high radial rigidity and load capacity, supporting the work rolls and carrying rolling forces in a very small space. The outer ring can be ground to size to keep strip thickness accurate.

FAQ

Common questions about this bearing type

What sizes do you supply in backing bearings?

The sizes listed on this page cover bore d 31.75–100 mm · outside D 76.2–225 mm · width B 26–130 mm · height H 22.2–65 mm (range of the sizes listed on this page; other sizes to order). Larger and intermediate sizes are made to drawing — send us d / D / B and the load case and we will confirm availability in writing.

Can you deliver precision classes P6 or P5?

P0 as standard; P6 / P5 available to order (P4 for precision types). Tell us the class required on the drawing and we will quote it as a separate line item.

Do you accept non-standard and small-quantity orders?

Yes. Sample orders start at one piece and non-standard bearings are made to drawing or sample; price, lead time and payment terms are confirmed in writing before any payment.
