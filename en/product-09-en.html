Single Row Tapered Roller Bearings

Tapered Roller Bearings

Single row tapered roller bearings take combined radial and one-direction axial loads; separable rings allow independent mounting. Pairs with preset clearance are standard for wheel hubs, spindles, gearboxes and differential supports.
