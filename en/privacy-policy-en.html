Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · Xingtai Hengtuo Transmission Technology Co., Ltd. (trading as HUOSIDUN, huosidun.com.cn)

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect only what you type into the form: your name, phone / WhatsApp number, company name, email address, and the requirement text (bearing number, size, quantity, standard). If the form is on a product page, we also record which product page it came from.

How we use it

Your details are used for one purpose only: preparing a quotation and replying to your enquiry. We may also use the email or WhatsApp number you gave us to send technical documents, drawings or a quotation.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with our own sales and logistics staff, and with carriers or customs brokers when a shipment has to be arranged.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send your request to zzq@huosidun.com.cn and we will action it within 30 days.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics to understand which pages visitors use. These tools set cookies and record anonymised statistics such as page views and clicks on our phone, email and WhatsApp links. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to any part of this site.
