Thrust Ball Bearings

Thrust Bearings

Composed of a shaft washer, housing washer and ball-and-cage assembly, thrust ball bearings take one-direction axial loads only. Separable parts mount independently — crane hooks, vertical pumps, lathe tailstocks.
