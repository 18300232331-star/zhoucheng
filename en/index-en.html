About Us

We are a precision bearing manufacturer focused on high-end, import-substituting products with precision classes P0, P6 and P5. The plant runs a full production chain — forging, turning, heat treatment, grinding and assembly — with precision CNC grinders, superfinishing machines and a constant-temperature grinding and assembly workshop, giving an annual capacity of 5 million units.

Certified to ISO 9001:2015, our physical & chemical testing centre covers chemical analysis, hardness, metallography, roundness and surface roughness. With nearly 20 years of experience in precision bearing R&D and manufacturing, we are a strong partner for custom and non-standard bearing solutions.
