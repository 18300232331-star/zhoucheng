Double Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings

Cylindrical Roller Bearings

Double row (NN/NNU) cylindrical roller bearings combine high rigidity with a small radial section and precision up to class P4/P2 — the first choice for machine-tool spindles and high-speed precision equipment. The ribbed inner ring allows precise preload adjustment of radial clearance.
