32/33M Series Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings

Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings

32/33M series use solid brass cages with back-to-back ball rows to take bidirectional axial loads and overturning moments — wind main shafts, pumps and slewing positions on construction machinery.
