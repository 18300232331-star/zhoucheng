Linear Ball Bearings

More Bearing Types

Linear ball bearings guide a shaft in straight-line motion with low friction, using recirculating ball rows inside a hardened outer sleeve. The LBBR series is the compact ISO series 1 design; the LBCR series is the ISO series 3 design with higher load capacity. Shielded and double-lip-sealed variants are available; open, self-aligning and heavy-duty designs are supplied on request. Typical applications: automation equipment, machine tools, packaging lines and precision positioning stages.
