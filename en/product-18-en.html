Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings

Angular Contact Ball Bearings

They carry combined radial and one-direction axial loads; larger contact angles raise axial capacity. Paired (DB/DF/DT) they give precise bidirectional location with high speed capability — machine-tool spindles, pumps, compressors and wheel hubs.
