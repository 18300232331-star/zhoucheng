ตลับลูกกลิ้งทรงกลม

Model & size range

Spherical roller bearings have a spherical outer raceway for excellent self-alignment, compensating shaft deflection and mounting error while carrying heavy radial loads and axial loads in both directions. We supply CA, CC, E, E1, MA and MB series in precision classes P0/P6/P5, with outside diameters up to 600 mm, for wind gearboxes, mining machinery, rolling mills and large motors.

ตลับลูกกลิ้งทรงกระบอก

Model & size range

Line contact between rollers and raceways gives far higher radial capacity and rigidity than same-size ball bearings, with separable rings for easy mounting. The range covers single row (NU/NJ/NUP), double row (NN/NNU) and four row designs; precision double row types reach class P4, and four row types are the standard roll-neck bearing for mills.
